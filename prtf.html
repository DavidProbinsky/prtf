PRTF — Physical Red Team Framework

PRTF is an open taxonomy of the tactics and techniques used in authorized physical red team engagements, from initial reconnaissance to sustained access. Think MITRE ATT&CK®, for physical security.

Click any technique to read its description, tools, detections and countermeasures. Use + (or Add to path in a technique) to build an engagement path, then copy or download it. PRTF is free, open and maintained by RedTeam.vip. Technique IDs are permanent and never reused. PRTF is inspired by MITRE ATT&CK® and is not affiliated with or endorsed by MITRE. ATT&CK is a registered trademark of The MITRE Corporation. The full dataset is on GitHub.
